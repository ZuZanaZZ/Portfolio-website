About me

Recently graduated with a Bachelor of Computer Science, where I found interest in both the theoretical aspects of this field, like learning about automata, and the practical part of making the theory come to life through coding, which I did by developing a game around automata in Python for my thesis.

Through my studies I've experienced and played around with some of the following: Languages: - Common Lisp, Python, C#, C, SQL, HTML, CSS & Assembly Technologies: - PostgreSQL, .NET & Pygame

When my creativity isn't applied to Computer Science, I spend my creativity on various crafts and arts in my spare time, such as painting, knitting, crocheting, cooking/baking & music. Other hobbies include social activities, working out & caring for my plants.

Throughout my life I've travelled a decent amount, participated in online communities and met people internationally. Making social connections across cultural, ethnic, international, religious and other kind of differences has taught me a lot, and I hope I can continue this journey, to get to know more people, and expand my interpersonal skills further!

Born in Slovakia, studied in Czech Republic and am now aiming for settling down in Denmark, where I've made several friends and found a partner.
